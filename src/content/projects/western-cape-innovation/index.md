## What it is

A metropolitan diagnostic of where innovation happens in the Western Cape, covering **11 municipalities** including Cape Town and Stellenbosch.

## What I did

- Structured **urban, economic and research data blocks** for all 11 municipalities.
- Worked with **NAICS-classified business data** and university research output from **UCT, Stellenbosch, UWC and CPUT**.
- Computed KPIs such as **Revealed Comparative Advantage** and innovation intensity.
- Wrote the project's **midway and progress reports**.
