## What it is

A hedonic price model: a way to break a home's price down into the value of its characteristics and its location.

## What I did

- Modelled residential prices with **OLS regression on log-transformed variables**.
- Used the model to **explain price drivers** across the territory.
- Fed the results into the **housing workstream of the Catalan digital twin**.
