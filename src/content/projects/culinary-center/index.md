## Site

The centre starts from the Pavilion of the Republic and its courtyard, and stitches them to the hillside below, where the old allotments return as a public park.

## Plan

Parallel earth walls run along the plinth and organise the whole centre. Small courtyards between them bring daylight and cross-ventilation into the buried rooms.

## Roof

The roof becomes a lookout square over the city, the first step into the new park.

## Construction & energy

Walls of compacted earth from the site itself. Their thermal mass and moisture regulation cut the building's energy demand almost in half.
