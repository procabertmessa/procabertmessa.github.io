## What it is

City Digital Twin is an interactive model of Catalonia built by Aretian Urban Analytics and Design, with support from the Torras Family Foundation and Barcelona Global. For each census section it computes indicators on housing, urban form, economics, mobility and research, so that places can be read, compared and understood at the scale a decision requires.

## In numbers

- **5,143** census sections
- **100+** indicators per section
- **2.9 million** cadastral parcels
- **1.9 million** building footprints
