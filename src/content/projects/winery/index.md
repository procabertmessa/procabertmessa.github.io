## Site

Plà de l'Avellà is a small plateau of vineyards in the Penedès, set apart from the main wine plain and bounded by white-pine forest. The winery sits on a knoll at the forest edge, next to the vines.

## Placement

Three criteria chose the spot: a raised position for views over the landscape, a north orientation for the production spaces, and a slope steep enough to hold courtyards at different levels.

## Courtyards

Two courtyards organise the building. A service courtyard serves the guests, restaurant and guesthouse; a production courtyard drives the wine-making, which runs down through the levels.

## Construction

Brick pilasters carry ceramic vaults, wrapped in GRC panels. Underfloor heating, solar and photovoltaic panels on the roof complete the system.
