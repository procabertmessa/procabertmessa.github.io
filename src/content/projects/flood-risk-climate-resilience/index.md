## What it is

A framework inside the Barcelona metropolitan digital twin for understanding where water goes when it rains hard, and where the city could hold it back.

## What I did

- Designed a framework for the digital twin to **simulate flood risk** and **flag candidate parcels for SUDS** (sustainable drainage) and water-retention systems.
- Ingested **ACA and ICGC layers** through **OGC WFS/WMS** services using OWSLib and ogr2ogr.
- Authored **10 climate-resilience recommendations** (7 on heat, 3 on flooding) for a **Puig Foundation** climate project.
